Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026.

The Decline Room app is a private tool used only by the owner of The Decline Room YouTube channel.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to read the channel's own analytics and to upload and manage the channel's own videos.

What it stores

Analytics reports and sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The app also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
